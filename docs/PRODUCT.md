# Product specification (MVP)

## Audience
Children aged 5–7 with adult support, starting in Côte d'Ivoire and designed for global access.

## Core scope
Five reference modules: M01 Seeds, M02 Sink/Float, M03 Levers, M04 Robots, M05 Counting. Each requires FR/EN objective, lesson, hands-on activity, materials, adult note, assessment, and eventual reviewed video and printables. Current app is a functional **text-and-quiz scaffold**, not a completed curriculum or video library.

## African languages
Baoulé, Dioula, Twi, Swahili. Stage 1: optional reviewed vocabulary/audio embedded in lessons. Stage 2: dedicated language tracks after research and pilot validation. No invented translations or AI-only linguistic validation.

## Design principles
Joyful, calm, accessible, responsive, simple navigation, adult supervision for physical experiments. No child account or personal data collection in MVP.

## Release gates
Educator accuracy review, language review, child safety and privacy review, rights review, mobile usability, tests passing, explicit founder approval.

## Privacy
The origin of the NATOGOMAN name is private. Do not place family details in public project materials.
